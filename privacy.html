Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how Kcolours handles business contact information and information accessed while providing marketplace and related operational services.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the service scope, we may process business contact details, product information, catalog data, account-operational information and documents provided directly by authorized clients. We do not publicly sell or disclose client information.

3. Amazon Information

Access to Amazon information is limited to authorized personnel and is used only for the approved service scope. Kcolours applies role-based access practices, multi-factor authentication where supported, password controls and prompt access removal when responsibilities change.

4. Personally Identifiable Information

Kcolours does not intentionally retain Amazon customer personally identifiable information outside the systems required to perform approved operational tasks. Where temporary access is required, such information is handled only for the relevant operational purpose and is not used for marketing.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical controls, including restricted internal access, protected business devices, account authentication controls, credential management practices and incident-response procedures appropriate to our operations.

6. Incident Response

If a security incident involving Amazon information is detected or reasonably suspected, Kcolours will investigate, contain unauthorized access, preserve relevant evidence, remediate affected credentials or systems and notify Amazon as required by applicable Amazon policies.

7. Third Parties

Core Amazon account-management and compliance coordination services are performed directly by Kcolours. Amazon information is not disclosed to unrelated third parties except where required by law or explicitly authorized for a necessary business purpose.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Kcolours minimizes the storage of Amazon customer personally identifiable information (PII) and does not maintain persistent copies outside approved systems required for authorized operational activities. Where temporary PII retention is necessary, it is retained for no longer than 30 days after order delivery unless a longer period is required by applicable law. Non-PII Amazon information is retained for no longer than 18 months unless a longer period is legally required. Information is securely deleted when it is no longer required.

9. Contact

For privacy or data-protection inquiries, contact Kcolours at kcolour1@gmail.com.
